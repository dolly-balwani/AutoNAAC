NAAC CRITERION: 5.1.3 Capacity building and skills enhancement initiatives

The Institution actively promotes capacity building and skills enhancement across four key areas: soft skills, language and communication, life skills, and ICT/computing skills. These initiatives are designed to equip students with a diverse skill set essential for academic and professional success.

In the domain of **Soft Skills**, programs such as "Skill Set Analysis and Resume Writing" and "Eligibility and Resume Building" were implemented on January 10, 2024, with 85 students enrolled, facilitated by Prof. A. Nagananda, TPO, VESIT [Document Proof 1]. Additionally, the "Share & Care: Senior - Junior Interaction" session on August 13, 2024, engaged 99 students, connecting them with industry professionals from ISS, Carwale, and IVP [Document Proof 2].

For **Language and Communication**, the institution supports activities like the VESIT Literature Circle and the IPL Quiz, fostering articulate and effective communicators. The Language Lab attendance records [Source: 5.1.3FINAL WEBSITE 22-23 (1)_compressed.pdf] demonstrate active participation in enhancing verbal abilities.

Regarding **ICT/Computing Skills**, the Computer Department's CodeCell organized several workshops in 2022-2023, including "React Bootcamp," "Git Way to Open Source," and "Design to Development using Django," aimed at upskilling students in modern technologies [Source: 5.1.3FINAL WEBSITE 22-23 (1)_compressed.pdf]. A six-day STTP on System Design Flow using Xilinx Vivado, AXI interconnect, and Embedded System Design Flow using VITIS, conducted by industry experts from CoreEL Technologies, saw participation from 33 students and 6 faculty members, fostering an exploratory mindset in FPGA design and prototyping [Source: 5.1.3FINAL WEBSITE 22-23 (1)_compressed.pdf].

The institution's commitment to comprehensive student development is further evidenced by a consistent enrollment in these programs, as reflected in the data from `data\Criteria 5 CMPN Data 2024-25 .xlsx`, indicating sustained engagement with an average of 20 to 40 participants per initiative, with total recorded participations of 248.0 and 255.0. While explicit documentation for life skills programs like Yoga and physical fitness is not directly detailed, the breadth of initiatives underscores a holistic approach to student development.
