## 5.3.3 Sports and Cultural Events

The Institution actively promotes student engagement in a vibrant ecosystem of sports and cultural events, fostering holistic development. During the academic year 2024-25, students participated in a significant number of events, both organized by the Institution and at external platforms.

The Institution facilitated student participation in **14 distinct sports competitions** and **7 diverse cultural events**. Specific examples of sports participation include Football, with a substantial contingent of students like Aryan S, Aman Ali, Divit Wadhwani, Ayush, Ajay, Tanmay Bagal, Hannan Chougle, Arjun Silwal, Abhijay Das, Pratik, and Nitish Bhosale demonstrating their athletic prowess. In Table Tennis, Swagat Pinjala, Bhuvhan Jamwal, Milap Mahapatra, and Chinmay Desai showcased their skills. Chess competitions also saw participation from students including Nitish Bhosale, Anish Godse, Anant Paranjape, and Anup.

On the cultural front, students actively engaged in events such as "Rangoli Revelry" with Rujuta Bhor participating, and "Fables of Florence (Short Film)" which saw contributions from Soham Parab, Dnyanam Gala, and Athang Bhandarkar. "Afterglow of the Renaissance (Aftermovie)" featured Lochna Paryani and Harshvardhan Patil. Students also took part in events like "Vigilance Awareness Week 2024" in various capacities, including "Confluence of Cultures" and "Jeopardy" (Kamya Manish Gupta), and "Poster Competition" (Pranav Chandak, Mayuri Joshi). The "NSS VESIT RESIDENTIAL CAMP 2025" provided another platform for student involvement.

This extensive participation reflects the Institution's commitment to nurturing talent and providing opportunities for students to excel beyond academics, contributing to their all-round growth. The data on student participation in these events is comprehensively documented.
